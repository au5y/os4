Components

Every architecture is built from these Appendix C (road vehicle) and Appendix D (e-bike) components. Hover any variant id in a table to see this decoded; this page lists them all.

Reading a variant id

  • Road vehicle C5-P1-G3-M2-A3 = chassis C5 · battery P1 · charger G3 · motor M2 · autonomy A3.
  • E-bike B3-E2-G2-K1 = frame B3 · battery E2 · charger G2 · motor K1. Level 4 autonomy (riding empty to a rider) is built into the frame.
  • Removable battery B3-E2-G2-K1-S2 = the same bike with swappable packs and 2 spare packs per bike, pooled across the fleet (S0.5 = one spare per two bikes). Team proposal, not in Appendix D.
  • Architecture 12×C2-P2-G3-M2-A3 + 60×B2-E1-G2-K2 = 12 of that road vehicle plus 60 of that e-bike.
  • Feasibility: a road battery may weigh at most 1/3 of its chassis, a bike battery at most 1/2 of its frame. Every vehicle also carries 100 kg per seat.
