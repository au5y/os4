# OS4 grading rubric

Transcribed from the course rubric. Each criterion is linked to the learning outcome for its task (Q1–Q6). Points per task match the task weights in the assignment: 10 + 25 + 20 + 15 + 20 + 10 = 100.

| Task | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Q1 Stakeholder utility | 10 |
| Q2 Technical system model | 25 |
| Q3 Tradespace | 20 |
| Q4 Second operating scenario | 15 |
| Q5 Recommendation | 20 |
| Q6 Extensibility | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Q1: Stakeholder utility (10 pts)

| Criterion | Pts | Excellent | Very Good | Good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single Attribute Utility (SAU) Functions | 2 | 2 to >1.5: SAU functions are defined and valid. Includes detailed explanation of SAU validation process. | 1.5 to >0.5: SAU functions are defined with minor errors, and an adequate explanation of the team's validation process is provided. | 0.5 to >0: SAU function invalid or not defined. No evidence of a validation process. |
| Multiple Attribute Utility (MAU) Derivation | 3 | 3 to >2.0: A mathematically correct closed-form multi-attribute utility function is provided and based on the given data. | 2 to >1.0: MAU function is mostly correct with minor errors, and/or derivation is not easily followed. | 1 to >0: MAU functions are derived incorrectly or with major errors. |
| Multiple Attribute Utility (MAU) Results | 3 | 3 to >2.0: Accurate MAU results. | 2 to >1.0: MAU results are mostly correct with minor errors. | 1 to >0: Major errors in MAU results or evidence of a conceptual misunderstanding of MAUs. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellency in Insights | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by providing assumptions and rationale to justify methods used, and/or discussion about valuable insights that can be ascertained from the data. | | 0: No marks. |

## Q2: Technical system model (25 pts)

| Criterion | Pts | Excellent | Very Good | Good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Model Submission | 5 | 5 to >4.0: Created and presented a model that runs properly. | 4 to >2.0: Model is presented but does not fully run properly and/or possesses minor errors. | 2 to >0: No model is provided, model does not run, and/or model possesses notable major errors. |
| Model Documentation | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Model is documented concisely and all modeling assumptions have been detailed in an organized fashion, within the 3-slide limit. | 3 to >1.5: Model documentation, explanation and assumptions, and organization are adequate. | 1.5 to >0: Model documentation misses key elements regarding explanation, assumptions, and rationale on performance and cost design. |
| Model Equations | 4 | 4 to >3.0: All model equations defined in a readable fashion with correct units (within the 3-slide limit). | 3 to >1.5: Model equations are defined with some minor errors and/or inconsistencies. | 1.5 to >0: Model equations are insufficient, unreadable, and/or possess major errors or inconsistencies. |
| Model Validation | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Convincing demonstration that the model has been validated and discussion on the chosen validation process. | 3 to >1.5: Model has been validated with adequate discussion on how the team validated the model. | 1.5 to >0: Model has not been validated or the validation method is flawed or not defined. |
| Architectural Instances | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Table includes 5 unique architecture instances, listing all relevant variables and critical metrics. | 3 to >1.5: Table includes 5 unique architecture instances, but doesn't include all required variables. | 1.5 to >0: Table does not include 5 unique architecture instances and/or omits many of the required variables. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellency in Modeling | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by displaying exceptional graphical representations and providing assumptions and rationale to justify their models. | | 0: No marks. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellency in Architectural Instances | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by conducting thorough analysis of the architectural instances, exploring mixed fleet options, or discussing valuable insights that arise from the analysis. | | 0: No marks. |

## Q3: Tradespace (20 pts)

| Criterion | Pts | Excellent | Very Good | Good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tradespace Diagram | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Produced utility vs cost tradespace chart which plots all feasible architectures. No errors and the presentation of the plot makes it easy to discern key points and metrics. | 3 to >1.5: Utility vs Cost Tradespace plot is adequate, possessing only minor errors. | 1.5 to >0: Utility vs Cost Tradespace plot contains major errors. May demonstrate conceptual misunderstanding of building Tradespaces. Evidence that there are many feasible architectures missing. |
| Reference Architecture | 3 | 3 to >2.0: Indicated the reference architecture on the tradespace plot. Detailed explanation on the rationale behind the chosen reference architecture. | 2 to >1.0: Reference architecture is indicated on tradespace. Adequate explanation on the rationale behind the chosen reference architecture. | 1 to >0: Reference architecture is missing or does not provide a good comparison benchmark to other alternatives. |
| Optimal Architectures | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Table with three Pareto optimal architectures plus ref architecture and rationale, and indicated clearly on the tradespace plot. | 3 to >1.5: Three Pareto optimal architectures and ref architecture are indicated on the tradespace plot with minor errors. Each architecture is shown in a table with adequate rationale. | 1.5 to >0: Pareto optimal architectures are incomplete or lack comparison to the reference architecture. |
| Tradespace Analysis & Insights | 2 | 2 to >1.5: Excellent discussion with insights on which decisions (attributes) appear to drive value creation. | 1.5 to >0.5: Adequate discussion on which decisions (attributes) appear to drive value creation. | 0.5 to >0: Inadequate discussion on which decisions (attributes) appear to drive value creation. |
| Reference Architecture in the context of the Tradespace | 3 | 3 to >2.0: Excellent discussion on what the tradespace plot implies regarding the reference architecture and answered each of the questions in the OS prompt. | 2 to >1.0: Adequate discussion on what the tradespace plot implies regarding the reference architecture and answered each of the questions in the OS prompt. | 1 to >0: Missed key discussion points with regards to the questions in the OS prompt. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellency in Tradespace Plot | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by displaying exceptional tradespace graphical representations and communicating insights through the diagram and text. | | 0: No marks. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellency in Tradespace Insights | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by providing valuable discussion on insights that are uncovered through the tradespace and identifying limitations to the approach used. | | 0: No marks. |

## Q5: Recommendation (20 pts)

| Criterion | Pts | Excellent | Very Good | Good |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recommendation & Argument | 4 | 4 to >3.0: They provided a clear description and persuasive argument for the recommended architecture. | 3 to >1.5: Adequate description and discussion about why the recommended architecture was chosen. | 1.5 to >0: Unclear why recommended architecture was chosen and/or insufficient and flawed discussion on why architecture was chosen. |
| Recommendation Supported by Evidence | 4 | 4 to >3.0: Recommendation is based on evidence from and references to the work done in Task 3 and Task 4. The answer walks the reader to arrive at the recommendation with no logical flaws. | 3 to >1.5: Recommendation is based on adequate evidence and does a good job on logically walking reader through the team's thought process. | 1.5 to >0: Recommendation lacks supporting evidence or possesses logical flaws. |
| Dominated Architectures | 4 | 4 to >3.0: They identified dominated architectures and ruled them out, providing brief discussion on this process. | 3 to >1.5: Identified some dominated architectures and ruled them out. | 1.5 to >0: Did not identify any dominated architectures. |
| Real Life Applicability | 4 | 4 to >3.0: They provided detailed discussion on whether their concept seems like a realistic solution for surface transportation in the Kendall Square area. | 3 to >1.5: Adequate response to whether their concept seems like a realistic solution. | 1.5 to >0: Inadequate or no discussion on whether their concept seems like a realistic solution. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellence in Argument Forming | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by demonstrating excellence in argument forming and persuasion supported by evidence and graphics, exploring additional scenarios, and understanding the tradespace well to achieve desired levels of performance at cost. | | 0: No marks. |
| Above & Beyond: Excellence in Applicability Analysis | 2 | 2 to >0.0 (Exceptional): Reserved for submissions that go above and beyond by analyzing the limitations of the methods used and how to overcome those, and detailed insights on whether the architecture seems like a realistic solution. | | 0: No marks. |
